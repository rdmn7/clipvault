# Product

## Register

product

## Users
People who need a private, multi-device clipboard for notes, code, images, and files. Their primary workflow is quickly saving, finding, sharing selectively, and reusing material without leaving their current task.

## Product Purpose
Clipvault keeps personal clipboard material private by default while making selected items available across devices and to explicitly chosen collaborators. Success means creating, previewing, copying, editing, and retrieving a clip feels immediate and trustworthy.

## Brand Personality
Quiet, capable, and deliberate. The interface should feel like a focused personal tool, not a generic SaaS dashboard.

## Anti-references
Browser-native alerts, confirms, and prompts; rounded/pill controls; mismatched component styles; decorative icons unrelated to the product’s visual language; opaque file handling.

## Design Principles
1. Keep the user in flow with inline feedback and deliberate confirmations.
2. Preserve a single, recognisable Clipvault control vocabulary.
3. Make privacy and ownership visible without adding noise.
4. Prefer immediate previews and clear limits over hidden upload behaviour.
5. Use motion only to clarify state changes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Use keyboard-accessible native controls and dialogs, visible focus states, readable contrast in light and dark themes, and reduced-motion support. File-upload feedback must be available as text, not color alone.
